About Us

Moving Business Forward

MAMS ZANZI (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is a logistics company based in Zanzibar providing reliable import, export, transport, clearing and forwarding services.

We work closely with our clients to simplify the movement of goods, handle logistics efficiently and ensure every shipment receives professional attention from start to finish.
